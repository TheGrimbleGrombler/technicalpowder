Surfacing

Our lord and savior SPRK has yet another gift to bestow upon us all:
When SPRK's life reaches 0, it doesnt just reset it's stats and change it's type, no no no,
It deletes itself and creates an entirely new particle at the same position.
That should sound alarms, why you may ask? Because of the PMAP.
When a particle is created at a certain position, the game goes
"Oh, okay- I guess theres a particle at this position now."
Without ever actually checking to make sure its on top of the stack.

What this means is: We can have any particle functionally be on top of a stack whenever we damn well please.
(as long as we can convert it back to SPRK, and keep a stock of 4 of them to allow us to cycle them.)
You want a stock of 4 ideally, or a nearby FILT @ 0x10000001 works too technically.
Such that each frame one particle surfaces at that point in the stack, and is put back in at LIFE = 4
So there is always one SPRK particle at LIFE 1, 2, 3, and 4 respectively.

Multi-Surfacing
Surfacing is good and all... But what if it was better?
Thats right, We can do this as many times as we want within a single particle stack per frame.
SPRK doesnt detect it's life is 0 and convert back to base elements on frame start, no no no;
SPRK checks for that when it updates, meaning you can just shove more groups of 4 SPRK and systems to cycle it in a stack.
For a demonstration of this, take these particles in this order from top to bottom:
SPRK(PSCN)
CONV(WOOD) [tmp = PSCN]
SPRK(PSCN)
CONV(COAL) [tmp = PSCN]
SPRK(PSCN)
CONV(TTAN) [tmp = PSCN]
SPRK(PSCN)
CONV(IRON) [tmp = PSCN]
