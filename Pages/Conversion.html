Conversion

One of the most commonly used particles in subframe devices, CONV.

When CONV updates, it checks each particle around it in the typical order,
TL ML BL TM MM BM TR MR BR

Each particle that matches it's tmp, or the inverse if it's tmp2 is 1,
is CONVerted to CONV's CTYPE.
No particle stats are conserved during this aside from the position.
The newly made particle will update just as the one it was made from would have.

For the most part it's as simple as that, though there are some special interactions.
CONVerting to SPRK will have the original element as the CTYPE. (PSCN -> SPRK = SPRK(PSCN))
CONVerting to FILT (125) + 512*n will spawn the FILT with a tmp of (CONV's CTYPE - 125) / 512.
